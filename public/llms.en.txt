# SlopeWorks

> SlopeWorks organises corporate ski retreats and team-building trips in the Alps. We scout the resort, handle every logistic and run the whole week on site, so your team just has to show up. We work with teams across the Netherlands, Belgium and the wider EU.

SlopeWorks is a B2B service for business teams. The approach follows the customer journey: strategy and trip design, operational organisation and logistics, and on-site guidance from the first call to the last day. Recurring resorts are Sölden, Mayrhofen, St. Anton, Kitzbühel, Zell am See and Gstaad.

Who it's for:
- HR and People Operations
- Directors and management teams
- Team leads and office management
- (International) Dutch- and English-speaking teams
